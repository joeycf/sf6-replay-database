THE METHOD — HOW YEAR 4 STAYS CONSISTENT
1 ANCHORSample the canonical costume or signature-effect color (Ryu's gi, Kimberly's spray, Ingrid's sun).
2 NORMALIZEMove it into OKLCH L 0.65–0.93, C 0.09–0.19 — the vivid-pastel band that survives 12px chips on asphalt.
3 SLOTKeep hue ≥8–12° from roster neighbors; crowded canon families (four golds, three reds) separate by lightness steps instead.
4 VERIFYProgrammatic check ≥4.5:1 on --color-surface; lift toward white until passing. Zangief, Akuma, and Bison were lifted this way.
Next test case: Yasmine (Year 4, Aug 3) — anchor her reveal-art dominant, normalize, slot, verify, ship --char-yasmine.
